[b]Building an empire is difficult. Keeping it is harder.[/b] Conquest, diversity, defeat, and a weak army all pull a realm apart. Collapse is normal. So is the quiet that follows if you beat every war that was declared against you.

Imperial Fragmentation is a gameplay mod for Crusader Kings III [b]1.21[/b]. It does not replace vanilla faction, corruption, or trade files. It adds strain, unrest, and a way back to peace. It is built on the 1.21 county control baseline and the Silk and Silver economy: prosperity, trade markets, and county corruption.

The pace is the [b]Imperial Fragmentation[/b] game rule: Lenient, Standard, Severe, or Collapse. The [b]Fragmentation Affects AI[/b] rule decides whether AI realms suffer the same way you do. Players Only spares them, but AI neighbors still prey on an overstretched player.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/header_features.png[/img]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_realm.png[/img] [b]Conquest Strain[/b]: A conquered county is Unpacified. Popular opinion falls and its control baseline drops, so control stays low until the land is integrated. Taking land quickly adds expansion heat. Four recent counties is Rapid Expansion. Eight is Reckless Expansion. Size, foreign culture, hostile faith, corruption, and that heat become Stretched, Overextended, Cracking, or Realm Collapse.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_tribute.png[/img] [b]Looted Land[/b]: Conquest costs the county prosperity, and its trade market loses traders for five years: goods cost more and sell for less. Low-control conquests catch county corruption. A cracking realm loses prosperity every year, a collapsing one three times as fast.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_armies.png[/img] [b]Dispossessed Lords[/b]: The lord who lost a county keeps a pressed claim on it and a grudge that lasts twenty years. While your realm is exposed, an independent dispossessed lord may raise an army to take it back.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_vassals.png[/img] [b]Factions[/b]: Discontented vassals are pushed into independence or liberty factions, and they are kept from quitting the next day. Giving in, or losing the faction war, hurts more than losing a foreign war.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_peasants.png[/img] [b]Peasants[/b]: While Realm Collapse is active, counties are pushed into peasant factions that are ready to demand. Fresh conquests you hold yourself flare up in [i]Unrest in the Provinces[/i]: pay for garrisons, grant concessions, hang the ringleaders, or let them shout and risk a rising.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_child.png[/img] [b]Child Rulers[/b]: A child who inherits a duchy or higher gets Child on the Throne. Inheriting a collapse is worse.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_enclave.png[/img] [b]Enclaves[/b]: Land you cannot reach from the capital without crossing foreign territory, and that sits far from the capital, resists. A short sea crossing from your own coast does not count. Vassals there hate you. Counties you hold there yourself revolt faster. The warning is a message, not a popup.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_tribute.png[/img] [b]Tributaries[/b]: A strained, distant, or humiliated suzerainty makes tributaries restive. They can stop paying or fight a tributary independence war. Obedient tributaries do not break the contract.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_armies.png[/img] [b]Weak Armies[/b]: If one vassal outmuscles the liege, or several vassals each field half the liege's strength, the bold and ambitious rebel. Content, craven, humble, and honorable characters stay loyal unless their own army is overwhelming.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/header_breaks.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Lost war[/b]: prestige, legitimacy, control, and the Defeated in War modifier.
[*][b]Lost faction war, or accepting demands[/b]: harsher than a foreign defeat. During Realm Collapse it is harsher again.
[*][b]Over domain limit or too many duchies[/b]: extra strain, opinion loss, and faster schemes. Dukes are not punished for duchy count.
[*][b]Cracking and Collapse[/b]: the control baseline falls across your whole domain, and control recovers more slowly.
[*][b]The ruler[/b]: stewardship above 12 lowers strain and below 12 raises it. Diligent, Administrator, and Just help. Lazy and Arbitrary hurt.
[*][b]The government[/b]: administrative realms take 15% less strain. Tribal and nomadic realms take 15% more.
[*][b]Personality[/b]: ambitious, brave, wrathful, vengeful, arrogant, fickle, greedy, and similar characters rebel. Content, craven, humble, forgiving, and highly honorable characters usually do not.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/header_peace.png[/img]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/imperial-fragmentation/main/images/icon_peace.png[/img] [b]Hard-Won Peace[/b]: Be the defender in at least two wars at the same time and win the last of them. For ten years the mod stops new factions, peasant risings, neighbor attacks, enclave revolts, and tributary breakaways. Strain falls, and one conquered county is integrated each year. Losing another war ends the peace.

[list]
[*][b]Integrate a Conquered Province[/b]: spend gold and prestige on one unpacified county that already has at least 25 control. You cannot be at war.
[*][b]Harsh Pacification[/b]: quarter soldiers in every conquest you hold yourself. Control jumps and integration becomes possible, but popular opinion and prosperity suffer, and you gain dread and tyranny.
[*][b]Consolidate the Realm[/b]: swear off conquest. Heat and defeat burden fall, and strain is cut by 30% for ten years. Take a single county in that time and the promise breaks.
[/list]
